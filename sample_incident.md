# Sample Incident

## Incident Description
During a high-volume processing period, a work area experienced repeated workflow interruptions that caused throughput to fall below the planned target for approximately 45 minutes.

## Relevant Metrics or Observations
- Planned throughput: 320 units/hour
- Observed throughput during incident: 215 units/hour
- Three workflow interruptions occurred within 45 minutes
- Average recovery time per interruption: 8 minutes
- Staffing remained at planned level

## Known Contributing Factors
- Operators reported intermittent equipment delays
- Escalation did not occur after the first interruption
- No clear ownership was established for coordinating recovery

## Immediate Actions Taken
- Operations leader escalated the recurring issue
- Equipment was inspected
- Work was temporarily redirected
- Team reviewed escalation expectations
